# Changelog

## v1.0.0 — November 11, 2026

**First public release of the ChatGPT General Protocol.**

- `REQUIRED` / `NOT REQUIRED` human verification at the end of every phase, including the final phase.
- Clear separation between result verification and authorization to act.
- Explicit handling of blocking checks and unconfirmed test results.
- Interactive continuation where supported: optional feedback, live preview, synchronized Continue and Copy buttons.
- Complete copyable prompt fallback when interactive controls are unavailable.
- Contextual handoffs, decision tracking, and conflict handling.
- Optional MVC-inspired modular organization.
- Completion criteria to avoid unnecessary phases.

This is the **first official public release**; earlier internal iterations are not public versions.
