# Start here — no prerequisites

**Simplest option:** attach this ZIP to your conversation, open [`START_PROMPT.md`](START_PROMPT.md), copy its prompt, and replace `[MY REQUEST]` with your goal. ChatGPT should read the applicable files before beginning when it can access them; otherwise, it must say so.

## What happens next?

- **Simple request:** useful, complete answer with a human-verification status.
- **Complex request:** productive phases with concrete outcomes and an explicit handoff state.
- **Missing real-world test, crucial subjective decision, or authorization:** report the blocker, provide precise instructions, and do not pretend to have completed the work.
- **Useful next step:** optional notes field, next-prompt preview, **Continue — next step** and **Copy next prompt**, where supported.
- **Everything completed:** conclude without an unnecessary Continue button.

## Example requests

- "Help me plan a move, with a realistic timeline and budget."
- "Teach me photography fundamentals in five lessons and adapt to my feedback."
- "Draft and refine my application package based on these documents."
- "Compare possible approaches to my project and help me decide."

## Important

The protocol does not grant access to apps, websites, or files. Interfaces that cannot display functional controls must supply a **complete copyable text prompt**. If you move to another conversation, reattach any needed files and deliverables: names and links alone do not guarantee access.
