## Resolving new notes

1. Record the original note faithfully.
2. Identify the existing decision it affects.
3. Determine whether the conflict is genuine or only apparent.
4. If compatible, incorporate it without asking for needless confirmation.
5. If incompatible and blocking, explain options and request a decision.
6. Record the selected decision and superseded rule.

Do not invent consent, approval, or changes of priority.
